Very Low Moisture Carpet Cleaning

A newer technology: a cleaning compound mixed with water is sprayed onto carpets and worked deep into the fibers by machine. Dirt and grime are encapsulated and removed by brush or vacuum, with far less water than traditional hot water extraction.

Traditional hot water extraction Up to a day
Pro Master very low moisture 30 min – a few hours
  • Significantly less water
  • Deep clean, dirt & grime encapsulated
  • Faster drying
  • Ideal for homes, offices & schools
  • Less disruption to your day
